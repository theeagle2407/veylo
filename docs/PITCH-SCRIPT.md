# Veylo founder presentation

Target runtime: 2 minutes 20 seconds to 2 minutes 50 seconds at a natural pace. Time the actual recording; the spoken text does not guarantee a duration.

When someone buys a digital product privately, the payment is only the beginning. They may need another download, a corrected file or a refund. Most stores handle that relationship through an email address and a customer account.

Veylo asks a different question: can the buyer keep access and receive service by proving one purchase, without creating a shared customer profile?

We built a digital storefront on Zcash testnet. Each purchase gets a separate key generated in the buyer's browser and a signed receipt after payment confirmation. Together, they form a portable purchase pass.

The buyer can download the product, export the pass, and restore access in another browser. They can receive product updates, leave a purchase-backed review and submit a refund request. Each action uses authorization specific to that purchase.

Creators can register a store, publish downloadable products and connect a local Zcash wallet. Payments go to the seller's wallet. A seller-approved refund requires a second confirmation in the local connector before it sends funds.

We have completed real shielded testnet purchases and refunds. Our recorded acceptance flow includes receipt issuance at ten confirmations, browser recovery and blocked future downloads after a completed refund. The newer source also passes 61 automated tests covering authorization, seller boundaries, payment matching and uncertain payouts.

Private checkout already exists. Our focus is what happens after checkout: portable purchase access and continuing service without requiring a named buyer account.

Our initial users are independent creators selling design assets, templates and audio packs to an existing audience. We would begin with creator-owned product links, then measure setup completion, purchase completion and recovery success. A future hosted merchant service could charge for storefronts, storage and support tools. Demand and pricing remain hypotheses we need to validate.

Today, Veylo is a working testnet prototype. Refunds require seller approval. Purchase passes must be protected, and our live deployment depends on a local backend. We are clear about those limits.

The next step is to make this purchase lifecycle easy to adopt and independently test. Veylo's goal is simple: let a private purchase remain useful long after the payment.
